## Verification policy

Every externally discovered identity candidate must have one of these statuses:

- `confirmed_source` — directly supplied or explicitly confirmed by Choco.
- `candidate` — potentially related, but not confirmed.
- `conflicting` — evidence suggests the source belongs to another person.
- `rejected` — determined not to belong to Choco.

Only `confirmed_source` data may become authoritative identity data automatically.
